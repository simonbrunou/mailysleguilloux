# mailysleguilloux

The one-page site of Maïlys Le Guilloux, a wellness practitioner near Vannes. Visitors find her through local search and write to her through the contact form.

## Language

**Contact intake**:
The path a visitor's message takes from the contact form to Maïlys's inbox: checking it, turning away repeat senders and robots, and delivering it by e-mail.
_Avoid_: contact handler, contact endpoint, contact form backend

**Header policy**:
The headers the site puts on its replies: the browser protections every reply carries, whatever produced it, including the outside origins the page may load from; and, for the site's files, how long a browser may keep them.
_Avoid_: header rules, headers file
